# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in oxlume.com, in the Oxlume product or in any repository of this organization, please email **security@oxlume.com** with the subject **Security**.

Please include:

- what is affected (page, repository or feature);
- steps to reproduce the issue;
- the impact you expect;
- how we can reach you for questions.

## Please do not

- open a public issue, pull request or discussion about the vulnerability;
- access, change or delete data that is not yours;
- include real customer photos, personal data or access tokens in your report beyond what is needed to show the problem.

## What happens next

We will confirm that we received your report, look into it and keep you informed until it is resolved. When it is fixed, we are happy to credit you if you wish.

Oxlume is in private beta. Our security practices are described on [oxlume.com/trust.html](https://oxlume.com/trust.html).
