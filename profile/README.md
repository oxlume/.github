<h3 align="center">Your AI video host. Film nothing, post everything.</h3>

Oxlume is an AI video host that turns a small business's phone photos into weekly, ready-to-post short videos. It is built for cafes, salons, shops and studios that have no time for marketing. The owner approves every video before it goes live, and AI content is labelled by default.

## What the AI does

- **Plans the week.** A planning agent reads the calendar, weather, local events and what worked last month, then decides what to show and when.
- **Composes the videos.** A composing model builds short vertical videos from the business's own photos and clips, with text, motion and the right prices.
- **Reviews every video.** A reviewer checks prices, spelling, blur and platform rules, and adds the AI label before the owner sees it.
- **Learns.** Views, saves and the owner's edits feed the next week's plan.

## What the AI does not do

- It does not publish without the owner. Every video is approved first; autopilot is opt-in, per format, and can be switched off in one tap.
- It does not hide that it is AI. AI-generated or AI-edited content is labelled, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act.
- It does not ask for passwords or run bots. Publishing goes through the official Instagram, TikTok and Google APIs.
- It does not reuse your images for anyone else. Photos are never used to train models for other businesses.
- It does not invent things. The reviewer blocks invented reviews, invented awards, health claims and prices that don't match the menu. Creative mode cannot generate people.
