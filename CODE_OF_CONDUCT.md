# Code of conduct

Oxlume is built for small business owners, and the people around this project come from many countries and backgrounds. We want every conversation here to be useful and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or who write in a second language.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Criticism of work is welcome when it is specific and constructive.
- Stay on topic and assume good intent.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats or encouraging harm.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.

## Keep real data out

Do not post real personal data in issues, pull requests or discussions. This includes customer or staff photos, faces, names, phone numbers, email addresses, health information and access tokens. Use made-up examples instead.

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks this code, email **info@oxlume.com** with the subject **Conduct**. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove content and block accounts that break this code.

For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
